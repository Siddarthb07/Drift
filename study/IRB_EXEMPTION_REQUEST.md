## Summary
Participants view **fictional** patient risk displays (honest vs legacy UI) and answer comprehension / certainty / care-seeking items (~10 minutes). No personal health data are collected into the Drift product database.

## Category (Common Rule)
Request determination under **45 CFR 46.104(d)(2)** or **(d)(3)** (benign behavioral / educational tests) as applicable to local IRB policy — survey research with adults, minimal risk, no identifiable sensitive health information beyond optional Prolific ID for payment.

## Procedures
1. Prolific recruitment; consent checkboxes; Prolific ID for compensation.
2. Random assignment to `honest` or `baseline` arm.
3. Four vignettes with Likert + multiple-choice items.
4. Completion code; optional JSON download; server stores response JSONL without linking to Drift `users.db`.

## Risks / benefits
Minimal: possible brief unease viewing medical risk language. Benefit: contribution to research on honest risk communication. Debrief notes that the baseline UI deliberately reconstructs known disclosure failures.
