# Survey instruments (vignette study)

## Comprehension (1 point each)

1. The CVD estimate for a South Asian user in Drift’s honest mode uses:  
   a) White PCE coefficients b) African American PCE c) Race-free PREVENT d) An invented ethnicity multiplier  
   **Key: c**

2. “Health age” in the baseline UI is:  
   a) Levine PhenoAge b) A validated biomarker c) An unpublished heuristic d) CDC life expectancy  
   **Key: c**

3. The cancer card in honest mode shows:  
   a) 10-year absolute cancer probability b) A compounded relative-risk product as the hero number c) Individual lifestyle factors without a combined RR d) Genomic risk  
   **Key: c**

4. FINDRISC’s percentage in literature is best described as:  
   a) Exact personal probability b) Cohort rate for a score band c) Lifetime risk d) Lab assay  
   **Key: b**

## Overconfidence (primary)

“How certain are you that the number on screen is a precise measurement of this person’s personal risk?” (1 = not at all, 7 = completely certain)

## Care-seeking (secondary, high-risk vignette only)

“Would you recommend this person talk to a clinician soon?” (1–7)
